Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile games Owl Roost and Unjammed! (together, "the Games"), published by Chris Kurppe ("we", "us"), collect and how it is used.

The short version

The Games have no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and the Games themselves do not collect personal information that identifies you. Your gameplay data — puzzle stats, win/loss records, daily streaks, settings — is stored only on your device and never leaves it except as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Games are free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob:

  • On iOS, the Games ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and related data to measure ads and keep them relevant. If you decline, ads are limited / non-personalized — the Games work exactly the same either way.
  • Google may collect device information, approximate (coarse) location derived from IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and enforce its policies. Google's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and Google's advertising policies.
  • Ads never appear during gameplay: they are limited to optional rewarded ads you choose to watch (for a hint, or to refill a gas can in Unjammed!) and occasional ads between boards (at most one per three boards, and never in your first session). A one-time "Remove Ads" purchase turns off the between-board ads.

Analytics

The Games record anonymous gameplay events so we can understand how the games are played and improve them — for example: a session started, a board was started / won / lost (with completion time and whether it was flawless), a hint or undo was used, a daily puzzle was completed, a streak count, an ad was shown, or a purchase was completed. Events are tied to a random, per-install session identifier — not to your name, email, or device advertising ID — and are sent to our analytics provider over an encrypted connection. We do not sell this data.

In-app purchases

The Games offer one optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads", $4.99). Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store / Google Play; we never receive or store your payment card details. Your purchase is tied to your store account and can be restored on a new device using the in-game "Restore Purchases" option.

Children

The Games are general-audience puzzle games and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention & your choices

  • On-device data (stats, streaks, settings) is deleted when you delete the app.
  • You can withdraw tracking consent at any time in your device's privacy settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • Aggregated, anonymous analytics events are retained only as long as needed to evaluate and improve the Games.

Changes
