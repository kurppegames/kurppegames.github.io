Stuck, found a bug, or just have a question about Owl Roost or Unjammed!? Email us at kurppegames@gmail.com and we'll get back to you. Please include which game you're playing and your device model — it helps us track things down faster.

Frequently asked questions

I bought Remove Ads on my old phone. How do I get it back?
Open the game on your new device, go to the Remove Ads screen, and tap Restore Purchases. As long as you're signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account, your purchase comes back — you never pay twice.
What does Remove Ads actually remove?
The ads that appear between boards. Two ad types stay, because they're entirely your choice: watching a short ad for a hint, and (in Unjammed!) watching one to refill a gas can and keep a run alive. Those only ever play when you tap them.
Why did I see no ads at all in my first session?
That's on purpose. Your first session is always ad-free, and between-board ads are capped at one per three boards after that. Ads never interrupt a puzzle in progress.
I declined tracking. Does the game still work?
Yes — exactly the same. Declining just means the ads you see are less personalized.
How do daily streaks work?
Each game has a new puzzle every day. Finish it and your streak grows by one. Miss a day and the streak resets to zero — the puzzles themselves are always there, streak or no streak.
Can I turn the sound off?
Yes — tap the 🔊/🔇 toggle in either game. (There's no background music in either game, by design. Just sound effects.)
Do I need an account or an internet connection?
No account, ever. Every puzzle — including the daily — is generated right on your device. A connection is only used when you watch an ad, make or restore a purchase, or when the game sends anonymous usage statistics.
